  • WSL 2 loses all networking under Check Point VPN hub mode

    2026-09-12 · ~6 min

    Connect the VPN and WSL stops reaching anything while Windows carries on fine. Names still resolve, so it looks like DNS and isn't. The client splits the WSL subnet into two halves pointed at the tunnel, and a /21 beats a /20.
